Glad You're Here

Welcome to DunRite Construction Group. You've joined a team that takes pride in safe, high-quality work — and we want your first days to set you up to do your best. This guide walks you through what to bring, how onboarding works, and what your first day, week, and month will look like. Keep it handy and check things off as you go.

Your supervisor and the office are here to help. No question is too small — asking is exactly how you learn the DunRite way of doing things right.

How this fits together

This guide is your roadmap. The details live in three companion documents you'll receive and sign: the Employee Handbook, the Safety Manual, and the Drug-Free Workplace Program. If you'll drive for the Company, you'll also get the Fleet Safety Program.

Getting Ready

Before Your First Day

What to Bring

  • Documents for I-9 verificationAcceptable ID and work-authorization documents (e.g., a passport, or a driver's license plus Social Security card). See the I-9 list of acceptable documents.
  • Voided check or bank detailsFor direct deposit setup.
  • Driver's licenseIf your role involves driving Company vehicles.
  • Any required certificationsOSHA 10/30, CDL, trade licenses, or equipment certs, if you have them.

What to Wear

Come dressed for fieldwork: sturdy work boots, comfortable work clothes, and any PPE you already own. The Company will provide or confirm required PPE on day one. Dress for the Florida weather and bring a water bottle.

Where & When

Your start location, start time, and supervisor will be confirmed with you before your first day. If anything changes or you're running late, call ahead.

Your Roadmap

Your First Day, Week & Month

Day One

  • Welcome and introductions to your supervisor and crew.
  • Complete new-hire paperwork (I-9, tax forms, direct deposit, acknowledgements).
  • Receive and review the Employee Handbook, Safety Manual, and Drug-Free Workplace Program.
  • Site safety orientation and PPE issue/fitting.
  • Tour of the jobsite or shop; review of where things are and how the day runs.

Your First Week

  • Attend daily toolbox talks and learn the Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) for your tasks.
  • Learn timekeeping and how to report your hours accurately.
  • Work alongside an experienced team member who shows you the ropes.
  • Confirm benefits enrollment timing and ask any questions.
  • Learn the tools, equipment, and apps you'll use (including Procore, if applicable).

Your First 30 Days

  • Get comfortable with our safety expectations and Stop Work Authority.
  • Build working relationships across the crew and office.
  • Complete any required training or certifications for your role.
  • Have a check-in with your supervisor on how it's going and what you need.

04
Most Important

Safety Starts Now

Safety is our first core value, and it starts on day one. Here are the basics every team member follows from the very beginning — the full details are in the Safety Manual.

  • Wear your required PPE — hard hat, safety glasses, high-visibility apparel, and boots — in all active work areas.
  • Attend every toolbox talk and follow the JHA for your task.
  • Use only equipment you're trained and authorized to use.
  • Stay hydrated and watch yourself and others for signs of heat illness.
  • Report unsafe conditions, near-misses, and any injury — no matter how minor — right away.
You Have Stop Work Authority

From your first day, you have the authority — and the responsibility — to stop work if you see something unsafe. We will always support you for speaking up. No task is worth getting hurt.
