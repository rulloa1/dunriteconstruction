Read Now · Keep In Cab

In an Accident or Incident?

Stay calm. Stay safe. Then work down this list — in order.

  1. Stop & secure the scene.Pull over safely, turn on hazard flashers, and set out warning triangles if it's safe to do so.
  2. Check for injuries — call 911.If anyone is hurt, call 911 immediately. Do not move injured people unless they are in danger.
  3. Do not admit fault.Stay polite, but never say the accident was your fault or discuss who is to blame — with anyone.
  4. Photograph everything.Damage to both vehicles, license plates, the full scene, road conditions, and any visible injuries.
  5. Exchange information.Get the other driver's name, phone, insurance & policy #, plate, and vehicle. Note any witnesses and the responding officer.
  6. Notify your supervisor now.Call your supervisor from the scene — no matter how minor. They will guide you through next steps.
  7. Fill out the report — same day.Complete the Vehicle Accident & Incident Report (Form DR-VIR-02) in this packet within 24 hours.
Emergency

If anyone is injured, call 911 first. Everything else can wait.
