Drug-Free Workplace Acknowledgement

I acknowledge that I have received and reviewed the DunRite Construction Group LLC Drug-Free Workplace Program. I understand that DunRite maintains a drug- and alcohol-free workplace and that I may be required to submit to testing as described in that Program.

I understand that a verified positive test, a refusal to test, or tampering with a sample may result in withdrawal of a job offer or discipline up to and including termination, and that under Florida law a positive post-accident test may affect my workers' compensation benefits.

I understand I should report any prescription or over-the-counter medication that may affect my ability to work safely before performing safety-sensitive duties, and that my test results will be kept confidential.

Confidentiality & Company Property Agreement

I understand that during my employment I may have access to confidential information about the Company, its clients, bids, pricing, and employees. I agree to keep this information confidential during and after my employment and to use it only for legitimate business purposes.

I understand that tools, equipment, vehicles, materials, devices, and documents are Company property to be used for business purposes and returned upon separation. I agree that at no time will I remove Company property — in print or stored digitally or electronically — from Company control, or store it at any unauthorized location (including personal devices or cloud storage), without written authorization from Company management.

I agree to return all Company property on or before my last day of employment.

Fleet / Driver Acknowledgement & MVR Authorization

I acknowledge that I have received and reviewed the DunRite Construction Group LLC Fleet Safety Program. I understand that operating a Company vehicle is a privilege requiring a valid license, an acceptable driving record, and compliance with that Program.

I agree to wear my seatbelt at all times, obey all traffic laws, refrain from texting and handheld phone use while driving, and never operate a vehicle while impaired.
