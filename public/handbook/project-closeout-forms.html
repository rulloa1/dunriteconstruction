Florida Lien Waivers

Use the Statutory Forms

Florida lien waivers must follow the exact statutory language in Fla. Stat. § 713.20. The four templates below mirror those forms for convenience, but confirm the current statutory wording and have counsel review before use. Use the Conditional versions when payment has not yet cleared; use Unconditional only after payment is received and cleared.

C-1 · Conditional Waiver — Progress Payment

The undersigned lienor, in consideration of the sum of $, waives and releases its lien and right to claim a lien for labor, services, or materials furnished through (date) to on the property of described as . This waiver is conditional upon actual receipt and clearance of payment.
