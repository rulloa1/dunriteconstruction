About This Manual

This Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Manual documents how DunRite Construction Group LLC performs its core operational, project-management, technology, and financial processes. Its purpose is consistency: when everyone follows the same proven steps, work is safer, cleaner, more predictable, and easier to audit. These SOPs complement — and do not replace — the Employee Handbook and the Safety Manual.

How SOPs Are Numbered

Each SOP carries a number tied to its part: the 100 series covers Field Operations, 200 Project Management, 300 Technology, and 400 Financial Controls. Every SOP states its Purpose, Scope, Process Owner, and effective revision, followed by the Procedure, Responsibilities, and the Records or Forms it produces.

Document control

This is Revision 1.0, effective January 2026. SOPs are living documents. Proposed changes should be submitted to the Operations Manager, who maintains version control. Always work from the current revision published in Procore / the company drive. Bracketed items such as roles, thresholds, and timeframes must be confirmed before publication.

Field Operations · SOP

Daily Jobsite Reports

Purpose
Create a complete, contemporaneous record of each day's site activity, labor, conditions, and issues.
Scope
Every active jobsite, every working day.
Owner
Site Superintendent / Foreman
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Capture conditions at start of day.Record weather, temperature, and any conditions affecting work (rain delay, heat, wind).
  2. Log labor and equipment.Enter crew headcount by trade, hours worked, subcontractors on site, and major equipment in use.
  3. Describe work performed.Summarize work completed by area/scope, deliveries received, and quantities installed.
  4. Document issues.Note delays, inspections, visitors, safety observations, incidents, and any open items requiring follow-up.
  5. Attach photos.Add dated progress photos of key work areas.
  6. Submit by deadline.Complete and submit the Daily Jobsite Report in Procore by the end of each shift. The Project Manager reviews within one business day.

Responsibilities

  • Superintendent / Foreman — completes and submits the report daily.
  • Project Manager — reviews reports, follows up on flagged issues.

Records & Forms

  • Daily Jobsite Report (Procore Daily Log)
  • Progress photos

Toolbox Talks

Purpose
Reinforce safe work practices through short, regular, jobsite-specific safety briefings.
Scope
All field crews and subcontractors on every active jobsite.
Owner
Site Superintendent / Foreman
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Select a topic.Choose a relevant topic from the Toolbox Talk Library, prioritizing the day's tasks and recent incidents or near-misses.
  2. Conduct the talk.Hold the talk before work begins — typically weekly, and daily for high-risk work. Keep it brief (5–10 minutes) and specific to current site conditions.
  3. Encourage participation.Invite questions and discuss site-specific hazards and controls.
  4. Document attendance.Record the topic, date, and signatures of all attendees, including subcontractors.
  5. Submit the record.Upload the signed sign-in sheet to Procore.

Responsibilities

  • Foreman — leads the talk and collects signatures.
  • Safety Manager — maintains the Toolbox Talk Library and reviews records.
  • All workers — attend and apply what is covered.

Records & Forms

  • Toolbox Talk Sign-In Sheet
  • Toolbox Talk Library (topic source)

Field Operations · SOP

Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)

Purpose
Identify hazards and define controls before a task or phase begins.
Scope
New tasks, new phases, and any high-risk activity.
Owner
Foreman / Competent Person
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Break the task into steps.List the sequence of steps required to complete the task.
  2. Identify hazards for each step.Note potential hazards — falls, struck-by, caught-in, electrical, heat, etc.
  3. Define controls.Specify the controls for each hazard following the hierarchy of controls (eliminate, substitute, engineering, administrative, PPE).
  4. Review with the crew.Walk the JHA with everyone performing the task before work starts; confirm understanding.
  5. Re-evaluate when conditions change.Update the JHA if the scope, crew, equipment, or site conditions change.

Responsibilities

  • Foreman / Competent Person — prepares and reviews the JHA.
  • Crew — participates in the review and follows the controls.
Cross-reference

JHAs support the Safety Manual. Detailed safety standards (fall protection, excavation, etc.) govern under the Safety Manual and OSHA 29 CFR 1926.

Records & Forms

  • Job Hazard Analysis Worksheet

Equipment Inspection Program

Purpose
Keep tools, equipment, and machinery safe and serviceable through routine inspection.
Scope
All Company-owned and rented equipment and powered tools.
Owner
Equipment Manager / Operators
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Ensure subcontractors are qualified, insured, contracted, and coordinated.
Scope
All subcontractors engaged on Company projects.
Owner

Revision

Requests for Information (RFI)

Purpose
Obtain timely clarification of design or contract documents and create a clear record.
Scope
All projects requiring clarification from the design team or owner.
Owner
Project Manager / Project Engineer
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Identify the question.Field or office staff identify an ambiguity, conflict, or missing information in the documents.
  2. Draft the RFI.Create the RFI in Procore with a clear question, references to drawings/specs, and a proposed solution where possible.
  3. Assign and route.Route to the architect/engineer or owner with a required response date and log it in the RFI register.
  4. Track to closure.Follow up before the due date; escalate overdue RFIs that affect schedule or cost.
  5. Distribute the answer.Share the response with affected field and trade partners and note any cost/schedule impact (which may trigger a Change Order, SOP 203).

Responsibilities

  • Project Engineer — drafts, logs, and tracks RFIs.
  • Project Manager — reviews, escalates, and evaluates impacts.

Records & Forms

  • RFI (Procore)
  • RFI Register / Log

Scope
All shop drawings, product data, and samples required by the specifications.
Owner
Project Engineer
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

Change Orders

Purpose
Document and price changes to scope, cost, or schedule and obtain authorization before proceeding.
Scope
Any change to contract scope, price, or time.
Owner
Project Manager
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Identify the change.Recognize a change from an RFI response, owner request, field condition, or design revision.
  2. Prepare a PCO.Create a Potential Change Order with a detailed scope description and price (labor, material, equipment, subs, markup) and schedule impact.
  3. Submit for approval.Submit the PCO to the owner/architect for written approval.
  4. Do not proceed without authorization.Except for documented emergencies, do not perform changed work until written authorization is received.
  5. Execute and log.Issue the executed Change Order, update the contract value and schedule, and log it in the Change Order register.

Responsibilities

  • Project Manager — prices, submits, and executes change orders.
  • Superintendent — identifies field changes and supports pricing.

Records & Forms

  • Change Order Log

Keep the project schedule current and use it to manage the work proactively.
Scope
All projects with a baseline schedule.

Project Manager
Revision

Purpose
Complete the project's contractual, financial, and documentation obligations.
Scope
All projects approaching substantial and final completion.
Owner
Project Manager
Revision

Scope

Revision

AI Usage Policy

Purpose
Allow responsible use of AI tools while protecting confidential information and work quality.
Scope
All employees using AI tools for Company work.
Owner
Operations Manager
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Use approved tools only.Use AI tools approved by the Company for business purposes.
  2. Never input confidential data.Do not enter client information, pricing, bids, personal data, or proprietary Company information into public/consumer AI tools.
  3. Verify all output.Treat AI output as a draft. Verify facts, code references, calculations, and contract language before relying on or sending them.
  4. Keep a human in the loop.AI does not make decisions on safety, contracts, employment, or money — a responsible person reviews and approves.
  5. Disclose where required.Disclose AI use when a client, contract, or law requires it.

Responsibilities

  • Operations Manager — maintains the approved-tools list and reviews use.
  • All employees — protect data and verify output.

Document Retention

Purpose
Retain, store, and dispose of records consistently and in compliance with legal requirements.
Scope
All Company business records, physical and electronic.
Owner
Office Manager
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure

  1. Classify records.Identify the record type (project, financial, HR, safety, tax) and its required retention period.
  2. Store securely.Store records in the designated systems with appropriate access controls and backups.
  3. Honor legal holds.When litigation or an audit is anticipated, suspend destruction of related records immediately.
  4. Dispose properly.After the retention period and absent a hold, dispose of records securely (shred physical, securely delete electronic).
Confirm before publishing

Retention periods vary by record type and law (employment, tax, OSHA, contract). Confirm the Company's retention schedule with counsel and the CPA before finalizing.

Responsibilities

  • Office Manager — maintains the retention schedule and oversees disposal.
  • All employees — follow the schedule and legal holds.

Scope
All employees issued a Company card.
Owner
Controller
Revision
1.0 — Effective January 2026

Procedure
